About Us

EduCoreX

The first EdTech platform developed by the Department of Educational Technology that bridges learners, mentors, and global alumni.

🎯

Our Mission

To make higher education more structured, accessible, and goal-oriented by providing personalized mentorship, resource-rich learning, and community engagement that empowers every student to achieve their academic and career goals.

✨

Our Vision

To create a world where every learner has access to the guidance, resources, and community they need to achieve their highest potential in higher education and beyond.

Our Story

EduCoreX was born from the Department of Educational Technology with a singular vision: to bridge the gap between learners, mentors, and global opportunities. What started as a vision has grown into a comprehensive platform that serves hundreds of students in their higher education journey.
